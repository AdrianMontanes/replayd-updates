Changelog

What changed, newest first.

0.9.18

2 September 2026

  • REPLAYD has its own notifications: a small card in the corner rather than a full Windows toast, with its own accent and a sound that is over before you notice it. Windows notifications are still there as an option on the Notifications page, and are the one to pick if you want them kept in the Action Centre.
  • Home is back. It opens on the last few things you saved, with thumbnails, and the sidebar now has three places rather than a list of fifteen: Home, Library, and the settings under a heading of their own.
  • Adding a game no longer means knowing what its window calls itself. "Add a program..." on the game list shows what is running and matches on the program, so the entry keeps working when the title changes mid-match.
  • Screenshots can keep the brightness of an HDR display, saved as JPEG XR. Off by default: a .jxr opens in the Photos app and in little else.
  • A drive that fills up no longer costs the whole recording. It used to: a recording writes its index at the end, and a file without one will not open anywhere. REPLAYD now stops while there is still room to finish the file, tells you it did, and keeps everything up to that moment.
  • Fixed a crash when opening the Library from Home.
  • Fixed REPLAYD staying in the tray after an update, for anyone who has asked it not to start there.
  • Recordings and screenshots that could not be finished are removed instead of being left behind as files nothing can open.
  • The window can be given a different typeface, on the Window page.

0.9.17

1 September 2026

  • Hotkeys work on controllers, racing wheels, shifters and button boxes. Hold the buttons you want and let go, the same way you would press a combination -- more than one at once works, which is worth having on a pad where every button already does something in the game.
  • Every hotkey has a second field. A combination at the desk and a button on a rig can both be live, so setting one up no longer costs you the other.
  • Fixed recordings that played their audio and nothing else. When the codec you picked was one your graphics card cannot encode, REPLAYD quietly used another and then described the file as the codec you asked for -- so nothing could play the picture. The file now says what it holds, and a setting naming a codec this machine cannot encode is corrected at startup rather than left to fail.
  • What a graphics card can encode is now asked of the card REPLAYD records on. On a machine with two it was asking the other one, so the settings page could offer a codec that would not work or grey out one that would.
  • Starting and stopping the buffer from the corner of the window reacts at once instead of a second later, where it used to show "Not recording" in red on the way.
  • Changing a setting from the corner now shows up on the settings page behind it, including while dragging a volume.
  • Save replay and Save clip both show how much they write in the tray menu, and Save clip has a hotkey field of its own.
  • Buttons on the Game list and Library pages no longer sit against the bottom edge of the window.

0.9.16

1 September 2026

  • REPLAYD now records on your graphics card rather than on whichever adapter Windows calls the default. On a laptop with switchable graphics that was the integrated chip, which meant capturing on the wrong end of the machine and being offered its encoder instead of the one on your GPU -- on some laptops nothing would record at all, whatever the codec.
  • Older NVIDIA drivers work again. REPLAYD was asking for a newer encoder interface than it needs, so a card with a perfectly good encoder could be turned away with a message telling you to update.
  • Fixed a leak that grew until REPLAYD ran out of memory. Every time it looked for a hardware encoder it left one behind, which went unnoticed on a machine where recording starts and mattered a great deal on one where it does not: there it retried every two seconds, forever. Retries now back off, and REPLAYD says which graphics card it could not find an encoder on.
  • The corner of the window has audio meters, one for what you hear and one for your microphone. The icon mutes, and the bar sets the volume -- the level shown is measured on what is actually being recorded, so it moves when you do.
  • The same corner answers as well as reports: clicking the state starts or stops the replay buffer, clicking the codec cycles through the ones your card can encode, and clicking HDR turns it on or off. None of them will interrupt a recording in progress.
  • The memory figure in the corner now matches the one in Task Manager. It was counting shared Windows libraries once for each REPLAYD process, so it read about half again too high.
  • Always on top takes effect when you change it, rather than at the next restart. Error messages carry a button to copy them.

0.9.15

1 September 2026

  • The window fits smaller screens. Its smallest size is now 1074 by 800, which leaves it room on a 1080p laptop at 125% scaling, and the sidebar was tightened so the page list still fits beside the recorder's state without ever needing a scrollbar -- even with a separate drive for replays, recordings and screenshots.
  • Maximised or full screen, that smallest size no longer applies: the window fills whatever the display has. It exists so the window cannot be dragged down to where its own pages stop working, and there is no dragging in full screen.
  • The corner of the sidebar reads a point larger throughout, and the sidebar itself is a little wider so drive names and their figures both fit on one line.
  • On the License page, the state and what it says about itself now share a line, so the dot beside it sits where it should.

0.9.14

1 September 2026

  • The corner of the sidebar now answers as well as reports. Clicking the state word starts or stops the replay buffer, clicking the codec cycles through the ones your machine can actually encode, and clicking HDR turns it on or off. They act on the replay buffer's settings, and never on a recording in progress: a recording owns the pipeline until it finishes, so a stray click cannot end a take.
  • HDR in that line says what the setting is, and turns green when an HDR file really is being written. It used to say what was being written, which meant it read "SDR" whenever no display was in HDR mode -- so a switch that was on looked off, and turning it off appeared to do nothing.
  • Always on top now takes effect the moment you change it. Turning it on only worked after a restart, and turning it off did not work at all.
  • The Home page is gone. Everything it said lives in the corner of the sidebar, where every page can see it, and its buttons were a third way to do what the hotkeys and the tray menu already do. The window opens on the Library instead.
  • In the Library, clicking one of several selected files now selects just that one, as a folder window does -- right-click still acts on the whole selection. Tabs no longer flinch when pressed.
  • The License page reads as one page: the state and the buttons that change it sit together, every button is one width with its label centred, and each state that needs something done has one highlighted button that does it. Lifetime licenses say so plainly, and there is a Check now button for when something has just changed on the licensing side.

0.9.13

31 August 2026

  • REPLAYD is becoming a subscription, and this version carries the whole of the machinery, switched on -- this build only reaches testers, and trial clocks burned while testing are reset at the real release. Nothing about the recorder itself changed.
  • A free 14-day trial starts on its own the first time REPLAYD can reach the licensing server: nothing to click, nothing to type, no email, no account. The corner of the window counts the days. When it ends, recording stops and says so; your replays, your recordings and every setting stay yours and stay open.
  • The License page, under Settings, is where all of it lives: buy from inside the app -- the checkout opens in your browser, and this device licenses itself once the payment goes through -- or enter a key you were sent. Your license key and Device ID sit there too, hidden until clicked, each with a Copy button.
  • One subscription covers one device at a time. Moving is removing the device here and activating there, one paste.
  • The subscription's own affairs -- the card, the invoices, cancelling -- live on Paddle's pages, one button away, so nothing about your payment ever passes through our side. What our server knows is a license key, an anonymous device name and some dates, and the privacy page stays true.

0.9.12

31 August 2026

  • Replays and recordings are told apart even when they are kept in the same folder, which is what happens by default: leave both folder settings empty and everything goes to your Videos folder, where a replay and a recording are both an .mp4. Both tabs were listing each other's files. REPLAYD now writes down which is which as it saves, so from here it is not a guess.
  • Files saved before that record exists are sorted by their name instead, which is right for anything written under settings you have not changed. Where the name settles nothing, the file is shown under both rather than filed under one and quietly put in the wrong place. Screenshots were never affected: an image among videos sorts itself.
  • The library can delete permanently, from the right-click menu or with Shift held. It asks first and says it cannot be undone, which the ordinary delete does not need to: that one goes to the recycle bin.
  • There is no Refresh button any more. The folder is read when you open the page and whenever you come back to REPLAYD, which is when it matters: save a clip, switch back, it is there. Reading a folder that has not changed costs nothing and changes nothing on screen.
  • Sorting, in a box where the folder path used to be: newest or oldest, by name either way, or by size. Newest first to start with. The folder path moves down beside the button that opens it.
  • The Replays, Recordings and Screenshots selector reads as tabs rather than as a row of radio dots, and "Play" is "Open" — a screenshot is opened rather than played, and one word should be right for everything on the page.

0.9.11

31 August 2026

  • Fixed: an update held back because you were recording looked like one that had failed. It sat there downloaded, did not install itself, and offered a button to install it by hand, with nothing saying why. It now says it is waiting for the recording to finish, and what installing it anyway would cost.
  • Fixed: "REPLAYD is already running" after an update, again. The previous fix could not work yet -- it was switched on by the copy being replaced, and that copy is always the older one, so it would have appeared one update behind for ever. REPLAYD now recognises the situation on its own.
  • An update that cannot be installed no longer reports itself as a download that did not finish. One is REPLAYD not getting the file; the other is having it and not being able to put it in place.

0.9.10

31 August 2026

  • The library is a folder window now. Every file has a picture of itself, and you pick things the way you pick them anywhere else in Windows: click, Ctrl+click to add one, Shift+click to take a run of them, Ctrl+A for the lot. Right-click acts on everything picked, and Delete and Enter do what they do in a folder.
  • The pictures come from Windows itself, so they are the same ones Explorer shows and REPLAYD decodes nothing to get them. A clip Windows cannot make a picture of shows its file type instead, which is what a folder does too.
  • An update only fetches what actually changed. Most releases move two files and leave the rest exactly as they were, and those are taken from the copy already installed instead of downloaded again -- about twenty megabytes a release that no longer crosses the network. What is reused is checked against the same signature as anything downloaded.
  • Fixed: after updating, REPLAYD said it was already running. It was: the copy being replaced had not quite finished closing. The new one waits for it now instead of giving up.
  • Pages change in one step. The heading used to change first and the rest a moment later, so on the longer pages you could watch one being built over the top of the last.
  • Importing settings no longer reports settings it did not recognise as skipped. It keeps them, so nothing was being skipped.

0.9.9

30 August 2026

  • Fixed: after REPLAYD updated itself, Windows' list of installed apps went on showing the version before the update, and would have kept showing it however many updates followed. Updating itself does not run the installer, and that list is something the installer used to write.

0.9.8

30 August 2026

  • Fixed: when REPLAYD could not reach the site and fell back to the second address, one of the files it needed answered as missing and the quiet update gave up, running the installer instead. The file was there the whole time under a slightly different name, which the second host had given it without saying so.

0.9.7

30 August 2026

  • REPLAYD installs for you rather than for the whole computer, in your own AppData folder. This is what lets it update itself: nothing can write over its own files in Program Files without asking for administrator every time, and an update that raises that prompt is one people put off.
  • An update can now replace REPLAYD's files where they are instead of running the installer again. Nothing new is launched, so Windows has no downloaded program to warn about, and only the parts that actually changed are fetched. The installer is still there for a first install, and for the times a file cannot be replaced.
  • Your settings are no longer refused when they come from a newer REPLAYD. An older build keeps every setting it does not recognise and writes it back untouched, so going back a version costs you the settings that version cannot show you rather than the ability to change any setting at all.
  • Updates can also be found through a second address, on completely separate hosting, for when replayd.download cannot be reached. Both are checked against the same signature, so neither is trusted more than the other.
  • Fixed: the tray menu could not be clicked, and nothing lit up under the pointer. It was drawn at one scale and asked about at another, so every click landed somewhere the menu was not.
  • Fixed: the dividing lines in the tray menu highlighted as though they were things to choose.
  • A check in progress says "Checking for updates" with moving dots, rather than sitting still and looking stuck.

0.9.6

30 August 2026

  • A Library page: everything REPLAYD has written, newest first, with its size and when it was made. Double-click opens a file in whatever plays it on your machine, and Delete sends it to the recycle bin. Until now, finding a clip meant opening Explorer.
  • Video was one page holding two sets of encoder settings, so neither a replay nor a recording had a page of its own. It is now Recordings, the replay's half has moved to Replays, and each of the three pages that writes files carries the keys that write them and the folder they go to.
  • REPLAYD can work the bitrate out for you, from what is being recorded: its resolution, the frame rate and the codec. The right number for 1080p60 H.264 is nothing like the right one for 4K60 AV1, and a fixed figure is wrong for one of them whichever it is set to.
  • The settings that decide a bitrate now say what they will cost. A replay length is not a memory cost until you know the bitrate, and a bitrate is not a disk cost until you know the resolution — so the page works both out and says so.
  • Two settings that meant something different at zero are a switch and a number now: the memory ceiling, and the bitrate ceiling, which has become a multiplier of the bitrate. Turning a limit off no longer throws away the limit you chose.
  • A new installation starts on HEVC at 10 bits where the graphics card can encode them, rather than on the H.264 fallback every encoder manages. Codecs yours cannot encode are greyed in the list instead of being offered and failing at record time.
  • An update found by hand now offers Download and Download and install, and does what you pressed. Automatically downloading and installing applies to the checks REPLAYD makes on its own, which is what those settings were always about.
  • Fixed: the tray menu opened away from the pointer, and could come up underneath an auto-hidden taskbar.
  • Fixed: restoring defaults ignored what your graphics card can encode, and left the Profiles page with nothing to switch to.
  • Fixed: a setting that could not be used was drawn brighter than one that could.

0.9.5

30 August 2026

  • Updates download inside REPLAYD now, with a progress bar, and offer an Install button when they are ready. The button used to hand the address to your browser, which meant a second warning about an unknown publisher — on a file REPLAYD had already checked against a release it signed.
  • The tray menu is grouped: Replay, Recording, Screenshot and Audio, each with its own folder to open. Starting a replay with options, recording with options and the three audio switches are reachable from the tray for the first time. It is drawn in REPLAYD's own colours and follows the light or dark setting.
  • Hovering the tray icon says what is being recorded, at what resolution, with which encoder, and whether your microphone is in the file.
  • Windows notifications can be limited to when REPLAYD is in the tray. With the window open it says the same things in the window instead, so nothing is lost and nothing slides over the application already telling you.
  • Microphone level is a volume slider now, where 100% is the microphone as it comes in, and system audio has one of its own. An existing level in decibels is converted rather than reset.
  • Settings pages: one field width per block, a centred column that does not rearrange as you drag the window, and shorter descriptions throughout. Storage's cleanup row is three rows, so the folder limit lines up with everything else.
  • Scrolling a settings page no longer changes whatever setting the pointer happens to be over.

0.9.4

29 August 2026

  • A Changelog section on the Updates page, showing what changed in the version you are running. It is there for updates that install themselves: nothing gives you a moment to read what is coming before it arrives, and afterwards this is the only place in REPLAYD that says what did.
  • A settings change that cannot be saved now says why. It used to report only that the file could not be written, which is equally true of a full disk and of REPLAYD refusing to overwrite settings a newer version wrote — and only the second is something you can act on.
  • Update messages carry fewer numbers: "You're on the latest version" and "There is a new update available", with the versions left to the panel below that already lists them.
  • On the Updates page, the switch for automatic checks now comes before the frequency it governs, and the download button reads "Download update".

0.9.3

29 August 2026

  • A Duplicate button on each game list entry, next to Settings. The copy appears directly below the original and carries its per-game settings, so a second entry that should record the same way takes one click and an edit to what it matches.

0.9.2

29 August 2026

  • Messages inside REPLAYD now appear as a card in the bottom-right corner, the way Windows shows its own, instead of a bar across the page. Lighter, longer on screen, and with a close button.
  • Fixed: the Updates page showed nothing under "Latest version". The text was there and unpainted, so it was invisible on screen while being perfectly readable to a screen reader.
  • Fixed: that same panel only refreshed when the page was opened or the button pressed, so a check finishing a moment later left it saying nothing had been checked.
  • Settings that only apply while another one is on are now greyed as a whole row rather than only at their switch.

0.9.1

29 August 2026

  • Updates can download and install themselves, if you want them to. Two new switches under Updates: one to fetch a new version as soon as it is found, one to install it once it has. Both off until you turn them on, and an installer is only kept if it matches what the signed release says it should be. Installing waits while a recording is in progress rather than interrupting one.
  • Windows notifications for updates, under Notifications. You are told when a version is available and what REPLAYD is doing about it — never that you are up to date, which is a question nobody asked. Clicking can open what changed.
  • A separate notification the first time REPLAYD runs after updating, saying which version it now is. Clicking opens what changed.
  • REPLAYD checks for updates within seconds of starting rather than after half a minute, and tries again shortly after a check that could not reach the site instead of waiting for the next one due.
  • Fixed: the record of which build last wrote your settings never moved past the first one. Going back a version was refused based on that first build rather than on the version whose settings would actually be lost.

0.9.0

29 August 2026

  • Audio can follow the application being recorded instead of the whole machine. Under Audio, "Only this application" records what the game plays and nothing else -- no notifications, no other windows. It also leaves out voice chat, which is why it is off until you turn it on, and it can be set per game. Capturing a whole display ignores it: there is no one application to follow.
  • A backup now carries your profiles and video presets. Exporting writes them, importing restores them, and restoring defaults clears them. They were left out of all three, so a backup restored a REPLAYD that looked right and had lost every named configuration.
  • Per-game settings can set the microphone: which device, its level, and mono. The replay length and custom duration have been taken off that panel.
  • Units now sit inside their fields, so a page of settings lines up instead of ending wherever the longest unit did.
  • The Open log button is gone from Backup. The log is still written to the REPLAYD folder in AppData.

0.8.67

29 August 2026

  • REPLAYD can tell you when a newer version exists. There is a new Updates page under Application: a switch for checking on its own, how often to check, a button to check now, and the version on offer with what changed in it.
  • A check asks this site for one small file and compares two version numbers. Nothing about your machine is sent, the file has to carry our signature to be believed at all, and nothing installs itself.
  • Older installers are kept when a new one is published, instead of being replaced. Going back a version is supported, and it was not much use with the older version deleted.

0.8.66

  • When a video preset is used by both Replay and Recording, changing a setting in one now shows the same change in the other straight away. The setting was already applied to both; only the value on screen lagged behind, which made the change look ignored.

0.8.65

  • The settings are still read, so going back a version costs you changing them, not the settings themselves.
